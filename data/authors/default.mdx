My research interests include human–computer interaction, human-centered AI, and cross-cultural communication. I use interviews, experiments, and prototypes to understand people’s experiences and explore how interactive systems can support them.

I received my master’s degree in Information Management from National Taiwan University in August 2026, advised by Nanyi Bi. I also hold a bachelor’s degree in Information Management with a minor in Philosophy from NTU, and studied Computer Science as an exchange student at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Sweden in 2024.

I am currently a part-time research assistant in Prof. Nanyi Bi’s laboratory at NTU, with an appointment from September 2026 through January 2027. I am assisting with a qualitative study of people’s experiences while waiting for AI responses.

In November 2026, I will join Prof. Nian-Shing Chen as a full-time research assistant, focusing on system implementation for a project funded by Taiwan’s National Science and Technology Council (NSTC).

## Research

With Nanyi Bi, I conducted seven hour-long interviews about cross-cultural mingling and developed an LLM-supported self-presentation ideation prototype. You can read the [Beyond Adaptation research overview](/research/beyond-adaptation) or our [CHI EA 2025 Late-Breaking Work paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706599.3719929).

My earlier research includes experiments with 15 participants on a changing-perspective projection system in VR, advised by Lung-Pan Cheng, and a rider arrival-rate prediction model for Global Express, advised by Ling-Chieh Kung.
